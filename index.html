Game Title Pitch & Concepts

Selected titles tailored to your core vision: space exploration, action dogfighting, wormhole gates, and a royal alien bloodline mystery.

Narrative Arc & Campaign Structure

From Earth Space Force Cadet to Sovereign Royal Scion. Action-driven pacing with low politics and high space warfare.

ACT I: THE EARTH CADET
Prologue — Sol System & The Crest

You begin as Kaelen Vance, a top-tier pilot in the Earth Defense Force patrolling the Jupiter-Neptune trade corridors. Raised by an adoptive retired mechanic who gave you a glowing ancient pendant, your life changes when a rogue alien raiding fleet triggers a dormant wormhole over Titan.

Earth Space Fighter Dogfight Basics Wormhole Anomaly
ACT II: THE VERDANT REACH
Uncharted Galaxy & Mercenary Warfare

Pulled through the wormhole into the Sovereign Reach, you find yourself thousands of light-years away. To survive and find fuel, you take mercenary missions for independent space stations—escorting freighter convoys, raiding pirate outposts, and upgrading your ship. Your pendant begins unlocking precursor technology inside ancient wormholes.

Space Exploration Free-Flight Missions Ship Customization Precursor Ruins
ACT III: THE LOST SCION
Reclaiming the Throne of Vanguard

The ancient precursor gate identifies your genetic signature: you are the last surviving blood heir to House Vanguard, overthrown 20 years prior by the tyrant Regent Vaelen. Uniting loyalist captains, commanding strike fighters and dreadnought fleets, you wage war across wormhole networks to reclaim your homeworld and save Sol from invasion.

Capital Ship Battles Fleet Command Massive War Operations Final Sovereign Showdown

Core Gameplay Mechanics Matrix

  • 6-DOF Dogfighting: High-speed maneuvering, kinetic/beam weapons, energy distribution (Shields vs Engines vs Blasters).
  • Wormhole Highway System: Fast-travel chokepoints that can be ambushed, explored, or stabilized. Unstable wormholes contain mini-bosses & precursor artifacts.
  • Small Ships vs Big Ships: Play in nimble strike fighters or board/command colossal Titan Dreadnoughts with turrets & strike bays.
  • Dynamic Mission Generator: Patrol, Escort, Recon, Base Siege, Bounty Hunting, Precursor Salvage.

Key Factions

United Earth Space Force (UESF)
Industrial, utilitarian human fleet with heavy railguns and armored plating.
House Vanguard (Loyalist Remnants)
Graceful, high-tech plasma-shielded cruisers and swift wing fighters awaiting their true sovereign.
The Iron Dominion (Usurpers)
Aggressive military junta utilizing dark energy armaments and giant planet-sieging dreadnoughts.
